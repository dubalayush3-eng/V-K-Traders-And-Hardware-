Inventory and security

The owner PIN unlocks everything. The manager PIN unlocks Products, Ledger and Reports, but not Settings, deleting or editing bills. Billing is open to everyone.

Staff

Add your staff. When the app opens, each person taps their name and enters their own PIN, and their name is printed on the bills they make. Staff can only make bills. A manager can also open Products, Ledger and Reports. If you add no staff, the app works as before.
