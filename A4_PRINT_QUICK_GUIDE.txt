❌ TROUBLESHOOTING:

Problem:         Solution:
────────────────────────────
Colors printing  Select B&W/Grayscale in printer settings
Text cutoff      Check margins (8mm), use 100% scale
Page breaks      Use print preview first
Bad quality      Increase quality setting, check toner
Text unclear     Use high quality mode
